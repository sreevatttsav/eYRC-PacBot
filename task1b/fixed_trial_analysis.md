# Fixed-controller validation trials

## `fixed_bounded` (first validation, 2026-10-16)

- 120-second wall-clock allowance; about 43.4 seconds of controller sim time recorded.
- 21,686 raw sensor ticks captured.
- Remained in `GATE`, with no state transitions and no recovery.
- No simulator verdict was received before the time limit.
- This exposed an overly restrictive 2 cm gate-probe cap. That cap was removed; the physical-contact guard and bounded recovery remain.

## `gate_retest` (2026-10-16)

- The retest produced 40 ticks (0.08 simulated seconds), then no further sensor frames.
- It stayed in `GATE`, did not recover, and received no simulator verdict.
- This is inconclusive; no claim of closed-loop success is made.

## `clean_start` (2026-10-16)

- Started the controller before the simulator; received 78 ticks (0.156 simulated seconds), then no further sensor frames.
- It stayed in `GATE`, did not recover, and received no simulator verdict.
- Also inconclusive. The latest change is not validated in a sustained closed-loop trial.

## `compat_run` (2026-10-16)

- Used the earlier non-`setsid` launch pattern: simulator first, controller second.
- An independent MQTT subscriber captured 16,355 sensor messages; the controller recorded 25,148 ticks over 50.296 simulated seconds.
- `GATE` reached `CRUISE` at 8.362 s; the first cell decision occurred at 12.580 s.
- A decision-driven 180-degree pivot completed with 1.227-degree error.
- Docking then stalled and initiated one bounded recovery. The 8 cm reverse changed estimated heading by about -3.2 degrees; the recovery 90-degree pivot completed with 1.804-degree error.
- Returned to `GATE` after the recovery pivot and remained there at the 60-second wall-clock cutoff. No simulator verdict was received.
- This shows sustained sensor delivery and first-cell navigation work with the compatibility launch; it does not conclusively identify why the earlier sessions stopped publishing. It also does not demonstrate a full run or solve the post-recovery gate-anchor problem.

## `recovery_reanchor` (2026-10-16)

- 49,951 controller ticks over 99.902 simulated seconds; the independent MQTT monitor captured 45,912 sensor messages.
- Reached `CRUISE` at 8.360 s, made a first-cell decision, and completed the decision-driven 180-degree pivot with 0.808-degree error.
- Docking stalled at 21.648 s; recovery backed up and completed a 90-degree pivot with 1.986-degree error.
- Recovery reacquisition crawled in `GATE` for one cell pitch, found no fresh landmark, and entered `RECOVER` hold at 42.364 s (`recovery_reanchor_distance_exhausted`). It did not start another pivot or invent a route.
- No simulator verdict was received before the 120-second wall-clock cutoff. The bounded fallback worked, but successful post-recovery re-anchoring remains unverified; this random run had no usable side-edge or paired-front anchor within the bound.
